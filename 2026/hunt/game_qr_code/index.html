    Rulebook

    Puzzmon, The Card Game: Official Rules

    2 players, 20 min
    Puzzmon: The Card Game is a tactical, two-player card game where you command mysterious Eldritch Puzzmon in a battle of strategy and luck. Each round, you'll select a commander, roll dice, and play cards to outmaneuver the enemy.
    Will you focus on powerful attacks, impenetrable defenses, or clever card control? Only the most skilled (and lucky) Puzzmon master will claim victory!

    COMPONENTS

    • 40 Cards per player: (80 Cards total)
      • 12 Commander Cards
      • 12 Puzzmon Cards
      • 12 Stat-Up Cards (4 of each type)
      • 4 Reference Cards
    • 3 Dice per player (6 Dice total)
    (Each player has a distinct card back!)

    GAME SETUP

    Each player takes:
    • One Commander Deck
      • 12 Commander Cards
    • One Play Deck
      • 12 Puzzmon Cards
      • 12 Stat-Ups
    • 4 Reference Cards
    • 3 Dice
      • Attack (yellow)
      • Defense (teal)
      • Special (purple)
    Setup:
    • Shuffle the Commander Deck and Play Deck, then place them face-down.
    • Draw 5 cards from your Play Deck to form your starting hand.
    • Reserve space for your In-play Commander and Stat-up Cards.
    • Reserve space for your Retired Commanders, a Discard Pile, and a Play Area for playing Puzzmon cards.
    There's no limit on the number of Puzzmon Cards you can have in-play!

    STAT-UP CARDS

    Each card provides +1 capacity to Attack, Defense, or Special when in-play.
    Stat-up cards can be stacked to save space!

    CARD ANATOMY

    COMMANDER CARD

    Synergy Effect: Ability that activates when the matching Puzzmon card is played.
    Stat Capacities: Base capacities of ATK DEF SPE stats.

    PUZZMON CARD

    Ongoing Effect: Ability that is active as long as the card is in-play.

    HOW TO PLAY

    The game is played over 5 rounds. The player with the most Victorious Commanders at the end wins.
    Each round consists of 4 phases. Players will act simultaneously for each phase by placing their chosen cards face down and revealing them together.

    PHASE 1: PLAY COMMANDERS

    • Look at the top three cards from your Commander Deck.
    • Choose one to be your Commander for this round, one to return to the top of your Commander Deck, and one to retire face down.
    • Both players reveal their chosen Commander simultaneously.
    • The Commander for this round provides:
      • Base stat capacities for ATK DEF SPE.
      • A Synergy Effect that activates if you play its matching Puzzmon Card this round.

    PHASE 2: ROLL DICE

    Roll all three dice simultaneously.
    Place your Attack (yellow), Defense (teal), and Special (purple) dice above your commander card in that order.

    PHASE 3: PLAY CARDS

    Both players play simultaneously for each play.
    Synergy Play:
    • If you have the Puzzmon in your hand that matches your Commander, you may play it in your play area and activate your Commander's Synergy Effect.
      • *Note: Playing a Commander while the matching Puzzmon is already in-play does not activate the Synergy Effect.
    Free Play:
    • Play one card from your hand.
    Special Plays:
    • Calculate the difference between your value and the enemy's value. If positive, you may play up to that many additional cards from your hand.
      • *Note: Only now does the Blackjack Mechanic come into play for SPE.

    THE BLACKJACK MECHANIC

    Each stat capacity is provided by your current Commander, the sum of all the matching stat cards, and may be further modified by your or the enemy's Puzzmon. Any minimum or cap of the stat capacity is applied last.
    For each stat, the value is determined as follows:
    • If the die roll is less than or equal to your final stat capacity: SUCCESS!
      • The stat's value is the number on the die.
    • If the die roll is greater than your final stat capacity: BUST!
      • The roll's value becomes 0.

    PHASE 4: ATTACK RESOLUTION & CLEANUP

    Pay attention to all Ongoing and current Synergy Effects—both yours and the enemy's—when determining attack success.
    • *Note: Only now does the Blackjack Mechanic come into play for ATK and DEF.
    Attack Resolution:
    • Calculate the difference between your ATK value and the enemy's DEF value.
    • If positive, your attack is successful and your commander is Victorious! Target one of the enemy's played cards for elimination (place in their discard pile). Then, place your Victorious Commander face-up in your Retired Commander pile.
    • Otherwise, your attack fails. Place your commander face-down in your Retired Commander pile.
      • *Note: Both players can succeed in their attacks in the same round. Some effects may affect your ability to choose which cards to eliminate.
    Cleanup:
    • You may discard one card from your hand.
    • Draw cards to refill your hand to 5 cards, unless modified by card effects.
    • If that was the 5th round, continue below to the Game End section, otherwise, start a new round at Phase 1.

    GAME END & VICTORY

    After 5 rounds (when there are 10 cards in each Retired Commander pile), the game ends.
    The player with the most Victorious (face-up) Commanders in their Retired Commander pile wins!
    In case of a tie:
    • The player with more DEF cards in-play wins.
    • If still tied, the player with more Puzzmon in-play at the end of the game wins.
    • If still tied, both players share the victory!

    Commander Cards

    Each player has one copy of each Commander card.

    Puzzmon Cards

    Each player has one copy of each Puzzmon card.

    Stat-up Cards

    Each player has 4 copies of each Stat-up card.

    Reference Cards

    ROUND OVERVIEW

    1. Draw 3 Commander Cards:
      • Use one for this round.
      • Put one back on top of the deck.
      • Retire one from the game.
    2. Roll dice.
    3. Play cards:
      • Synergy play and effects.
        • Synergy Effect can only be activated by Puzzmon Cards played after the Commander Card.
      • Free play and effects.
        • Choose any card from your hand and play it. By default, any Ongoing Effect it gives happens immediately.
    4. Play cards (Continued):
      • Special plays.
        • Calculate your SPE value minus your opponent's.
        • If positive, play (up to) that many more cards from your hand, activating effects.
    5. Resolve attacks and clean up:
      • Calculate your ATK value minus your Enemy's DEF value.
        • If positive, your Commander is Victorious this round.
      • You may discard one card, then draw 5 cards, unless modified by effects.

    BLACKJACK MECHANIC

    When calculating the value of any stat for special plays or attack resolution:
    • Add all sources of that stat's capacity from your commander and stat-ups. Modify it with any effects from Puzzmon in play. Any minimum or cap of the stat capacity is applied last.
    • If what shows on the stat die is greater than the capacity, you've "busted" and the stat's value is 0. Otherwise, the stat's value is the number on the die.

    THE END OF THE GAME

    After 5 rounds, the player with more Victorious Commanders is declared the winner.
    • Ties are broken by whichever player has more DEF cards in play.
    • Further ties are broken by whichever player has more Puzzmon cards in play.
    • Still tied? Victory is shared!

    Card Backs

    One player's card backs are teal. The other player's card backs are yellow. The Commander back and the Reference back are used for the Commander and Reference cards respectively. The plain back is used for the Puzzmon and Stat-up cards.

    FAQ / Game Clarifications

    Simultaneous Dice Rerolls

    If both players get to reroll a dice on the same turn, they must choose simultaneously. If the same dice is chosen, then it is only rerolled once.

    Reference Card Clarification

    There is a minor inconsistency on the 2nd Reference card under "4. Resolve attacks and clean up." It should say draw UP TO 5 cards like it does in the Rulebook.

    "This Round" Effects

    Any card that refers to "this round" applies to all rounds where that card is in play.
