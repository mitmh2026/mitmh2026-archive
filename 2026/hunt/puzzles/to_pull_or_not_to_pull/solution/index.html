    Solution to To Pull or Not to Pull?

    Answer: WEAVE GACHA COVER
    Written by: Shuxin Zhan and Benji Nguyen

    The flavortext hints at two aspects of the round that need to be explored in order to solve the meta:

    • Analyzing the pull history
    • Pulling to reach the end of each banner

    There are various hints for how to use the pull history. After a puzzle is solved, their icon on the round page becomes a circle or a vertical line. This visual cue, along with the meta title's and meta quest's binary questions, point to binary encoding.

    If we assign each clue in the pull history to 0 or 1, based on which puzzle it belonged to, we get a repeating clue phrase comprised of a word plus three numbers. The clue sorting and binary is shown at the bottom of the page.

    Now, we may have noticed that all of the paired puzzles in this round have answers that are the same length. The word in the clue phrase is a synonym for a word of the same length that is a mix of letters from the two feeder answers. The leftover letters form another word, which we can index with the three numbers.

    The second part of the meta requires us to continue pulling on the banners until we reach the bottom. This is clued by both the flavor text and the quest, which encourages teams to pull and gives them a very big budget to do so.

    Each of the banners bottom out at 156 pulls and drop the following shell pieces:

    BannerIndexed LetterClue at the Bottom
    ArtVCO
    WordsEEV
    CellsACE
    ChartsAHR
    CharactersWGA

    For each banner, we can place the three letters in the three lit up machines in order, which gives us the answer WEAVE GACHA COVER.
