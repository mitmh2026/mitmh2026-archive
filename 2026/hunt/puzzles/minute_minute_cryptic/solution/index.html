    There are two sets of 8 clues that may jump out to the solvers, also hinted by the 8 + 8 under the grid. The first is a set of 8 clues where the answers are all synonyms of minute meaning small: PETITE, MINOR, TINY, LITTLE, WEE, TOY, DWARF, and MICRO. The other is another set of 8 clues that contain the word "time", where the definition part of the cryptic did not quite match the answer that fits into the grid. However, if a time-related word is added to the grid answer, then the definition makes more sense; the position of the time-related word is further confirmed by which end “time” appears on, and this relates to the second meaning of “minute”: the time-based one.
