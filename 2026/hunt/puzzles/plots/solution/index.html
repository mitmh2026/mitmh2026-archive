    Solution to Plots

    Answer: SLATED
    Written by: Benji Nguyen and Shuxin Zhan

    This puzzle consists of six minipuzzles and a meta plot given in the shell, which appears upon puzzle unlock. Each minipuzzle has a meta plot with a red border, along with feeder plots associated with the minipuzzle. The main aha is figuring out which plots go with which minipuzzle group and why. Each meta plot resolves to an answer to be used in the final meta plot. The emoji in each meta plot title is a hint at that group's theme.

    Alternate Histories

Red lines in both the meta plot and the individual feeder plots represent an element labeled as the final one. The y-values of the meta plot are the initials of the final-type moniker given to the not-actually final entries in each franchise.

    Frame Stories

    This group of plots concerns stories that contain frame stories, some of which are quite complex. Values lower on the y-axis indicate lower depths in the story (more frames within frames). The outermost frame of each story appears on the meta plot, and we deduce the overall media based on the layers and images depicted.

    On the meta plot, we match frames to the correct story and index into the title using the clueful emojis below the frame. This yields the mini-meta answer IBERIA.

    StoryExplanationIndexExtract
    InceptionThe images represent layers of the dream within a dream in the film (the rainy city in the frame here, the hotel, the snowy area, Saito's palace).7I
    The Arabian NightsThese are (non-comprehensive) representations of stories that Scheherazade tells the king to prolong her life. The graph goes in and out as Scheherazade pauses and resumes storytelling each night.7B
    FrankensteinThe frame story of Frankenstein is a series of letters from a sea captain who encounters Victor Frankenstein and his monster. He recounts Victor's experimentation and the deaths (including the monster's murders) of various figures in Frankenstein's life.6E
    Forrest GumpThe frame story of Forrest Gump consists of the title character relating his life story to a series of strangers on a bench. This includes his successes at sports, shrimp enterprises, and time at war.4R
    How I Met Your MotherThe frame story of How I Met Your Mother is Ted Mosby gradually telling his children about how he met their mother. The specific moments captured in this plot are ones specifically about Ted and the mother (for example, the umbrella incident, their trip to a lighthouse, the wedding).4I
    Cloud AtlasCloud Atlas is told through a nested story-within-a-story. There are six layers and the book moves through each one down to the deepest level and back out through each of them (similar to Inception). The images show what country these layers' stories took place in.6A

    Reverse Chronologies

    This group consists of plots of stories that are told in reverse chronological order. However, the plot portrays the events in chronological order, with time increasing along the x-axis. The meta plot has six different y-values, each corresponding to one of the stories. The label for each story is formatted as the last letter of the story, an ellipsis, and the first letter, which is a reversal of the title. We index into these titles with the numbers where there is a point at that y-value and extract from left to right. Doing this yields the mini-meta answer THE RAILWAY CHILDREN.

    Video Game Bosses

    The plots in this group correspond to partitions of a game, where each partition typically maps to a boss. The exception is Sonic the Hedgehog, where some zones do not have bosses.

    TitlePlot Explanation
    Mega Man 2Each image on a bar represents a weapon used by a Robot Master in the game. The bar directly after it is decremented because it belongs to a Robot Master that is weak to the weapon before it. The plot cycles back to the start in the ninth bar because the weapon weaknesses form a chain.
    Persona 5Each image on a point represents a palace (or dungeon) in the game.
    Pokemon Red/Blue/YellowEach image on a point represents the name of a badge given by a gym leader in the game.
    Sonic the HedgehogEach image on a point represents the name of a zone in the game.
    Super Mario Bros. 3Each image on a point represents the name of a land in the game.
    The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of TimeEach image represents the award obtained by completing a dungeon in the game (for example, a Spiritual Stone or a Sage's Medallion).

    The meta plot contains clueful images along the x-axis. Each pair of images represents the boss archetype found in each game. The y-values tell you which boss to look at for the game at that x-value (two bosses per game). If N is the actual y-value, then we index into that game's Nth boss using the number over each point. For games where the order is not clear, like in Mega Man 2, we use the given order from the feeder plot. This yields the mini-meta answer THE GOOD EARTH.
