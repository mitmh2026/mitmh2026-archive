    Wedding Invitation

    The solver is presented with an invitation to a wedding. As implied by the signature, they need to navigate to withjoy.com, a popular website for wedding invitations and information. They can either search by keywords for “cardi” and “nality” or go directly to https://withjoy.com/cardi-and-nality. To unlock more content, they must enter the password, given by flowers arranged like braille: SITE.

    Now, the solver should see a series of pages, each with a minipuzzle. They will need to solve each one to then tackle the Finale, for which they can scroll to the bottom of the site to fetch enumerations and indices.

    Registry

The specific villager’s name can be disambiguated via the presence or absence of the ring emoji, which denotes whether the villager is able to marry or not.     Seating Chart

    The competitive family mentioned by the puzzle is the Bridgerton family from the book series/TV show, where each sibling has a name starting with A-H. Solvers must figure out the seating arrangement around the table and also how many truffles each sibling ate.

    One possible solve path for the seating arrangement is as follows:

    • Two eldest siblings are on the same half of the table as two youngest → eldest are A, B; youngest are G, H
      • <DEFC> || <ABGH>
    • Four siblings with even number of letters are all in a row → B, D, E, and H
      • <CF><DE> || <HB><GA>
    • Sister named after a flower is separate from other girls → H is NOT next to D & E
      • <CF><DE> || B H <GA> → this locks in B & H
    • Sibling with longest name is sitting to the right of sibling with shortest → F next to C
      • C F <DE> || B H <GA>
    • G is next to E’s friend’s partner → G next to C
      • C F <DE> || B H A G
    • D is opposite youngest -> D across from G
      • C F E D B H A G

    For the number of truffles, one solve path is as follows:

    • Boys combined ate 6 more than girls combined
      • 54 total → 30 for the boys, 24 for the girls
    • The two younger sisters each ate 3x as many truffles as their two oldest sisters
      • If they ate 24 total, and each sister ate the same number as another sister, this means they ate D: 3 / E: 3 / F: 9 / H: 9
    • B and C combined ate the same number as F and H , so they ate 18 combined
    • The three oldest brothers ate 5x as many as youngest brother
      • Their total is 30, so this means G ate 5 and the other three ate 5x5 = 25
      • We know one brother ate 1, so the other two ate 24 collectively
    • B ate 10 more than his older brother, so it’s either:
      • If Anthony ate 1: A: 1, B: 1 + 10 = 11, C would need to eat 18 - 11 = 7
        • But then 1 + 11 + 7 + 5 doesn’t add up to 30
      • If Colin ate 1:
        • C and G cover 1 + 5 of the 30, leaving 24 behind
        • B = A + 10, and A + B = 24, so A = 7 and B = 17

    Then, as hinted by flavor, we must look up the book that corresponds to the sibling in question (example: official Bridgerton author’s website). Note that the book order is not synonymous with sibling age order. The final answer is as follows, in seating arrangement order: RECKLESS.

    Author’s Notes

    Ali and Marisa were both bridesmaids for each other’s weddings recently. They figured they might as well transform all this newly obsolete information floating around in their heads into a puzzle.

    A few teams were able to see the original Registry puzzle, which used the actual WithJoy registry. However, after someone tried to purchase a gift off the registry (with a very nice note!), we had to close the registry and replace it with a static image.
