    Author's Notes

    Bugcat Capoo is a meaningful comic to me. Under its innocent exterior, it often tells stories about loss and unconditional love in a chaotic world.

    Unfortunately, I think the work has lost most of its gravitas since the end of the webcomic, and its move to short-form video and increased commercialization. I'm happy that the author is still active and loves his work, but I can't help being sad that the newer content doesn't resonate with me. On the other hand, the webcomic went out on top, and will always stand by itself.

    I hope you enjoyed reading the comics too! Some of my favorite strips are Stone Dog Statue, Lunch War, and those that celebrate selfless, invisible service (Light Bulb Bugcats, Bugcat Battery, Capoo Gachapon).
