    As we solve more clues, we may realize that the answers can be paired to represent a word transformation. As hinted by the flavor text, we wish to build a word transformation chain for Goku to transform into a Super Saiyan.

    In addition, the flavor text mentioned "his appearance" and "look more Super Saiyan", and the card is rainbow-colored. This hints at another clue which can be very useful for building the transformation chain.

    Goku's hair is black, and (the most iconic form of) Super Saiyan's hair is yellow. To look more Super Saiyan, Goku's hair color needs to change. If we check the hair colors of the characters, we notice that besides word transformation, each pair of characters also represents a hair color transformation. This helps us with pairing the answers and building the transformation chain: Goku starts from black hair, undergoes a number of transformations to change his hair color, and ends with yellow hair.
