    Solution to As the Fly Crows

    Answer: FENCES
    Written by: Sam Wang
    Edited by: Benji Nguyen

    These are cryptics, but with a key twist - “cryptic transformations” treat the words to be transformed as if they were composed as sounds, instead of letters. The flavortext is stylized as an "announcement" and mentions Ipota Airport, which has an IATA code of IPA, to hint at this use of IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet) in the puzzle. All the answers to the clues can only fit into the grid if we use the IPA symbols instead of letters.

    Example: “fewer and fewer sell with a return” requires a reversal of SELL (composed of the sounds /sɛl/), to get /lɛs/, the pronunciation of LESS.

    Given enumerations are for the word written in English for solver convenience, but the answers need to be filled into the grid with their component sounds (or written in IPA).

    Note that some symbols, e.g. “ː” after a vowel to denote a long vowel, are effectively ignored for the purpose of fulfilling the grid.

    The flavor text suggests that the solver should extract from 5A, 15A, 25A, 28A, 30A, 43D, and 44A (interpreting A = Across and D = Down). However, after filling out the majority of the grid, solvers will realize that these clues don’t currently exist on the grid! Solvers need to infer where these clues might be if they could ignore the bars (like a plane flying over terrain), while still being constrained by other crossword rules. E.g. 15-Across must start strictly after any of 14-Across’ cells, but can only extend up to any of 17-Across’ cells. These “flying clues” are highlighted in yellow below:

    This gives us the final clue phrase /ði ænsər ɪz rɔŋ fɪn sɛz sɪks/ “The answer is wrong, Finn says (6)”. The answer to this cryptic is /fɛnsɪz/ FENCES, derived from anagramming fɪn + sɛz.
