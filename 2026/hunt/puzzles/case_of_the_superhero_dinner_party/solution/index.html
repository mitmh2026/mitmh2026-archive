    Solution to The Case of the Superhero Dinner Party

    Answer: LONG TALL SALLY
    Written by: Benji Nguyen and Shuxin Zhan
    Edited by: Herman Chau

    Inspired by games like The Case of the Golden Idol, this puzzle is a murder mystery where solvers must piece together the events of a crime through different types of levels. Roughly speaking, each level is intended to shed light on some facet of the crime, similar to how cases in Golden Idol are split into multiple mad libs-style sections that might correspond to things like the characters involved, locations, organization hierarchies, etc.

    The solutions are not necessarily presented in logical solving order and each level description may reference information that's meant to be inferred from a different level.

    Level: Dining & Living Room

    This is a simple visual jigsaw. The main information to be gleaned from this is the location of the body, the location of the large present, and various clothing on the clothing rack, which may help identify the order in which guests arrive.

    Level: Kitchen

    As with above, this is a jigsaw. There are notes here about the shopping list and Captain Strong's allergy. There is also smoke coming from the trash can, indicating that Inferna used her power to destroy some item (specifically, a blender) and threw it away. There is also a photograph of the old Superhero Association.

    Level: Blueprint

    This is another simple visual jigsaw intended to teach the layout of Inferna's house.

    Character aliases and abilities can generally be solved by reading through testimonies carefully. The process of mapping names to aliases is relatively straightforward through simple conversation reading in the testimonies and transcripts. Some power explanations are below.

    • Hugh Danes: It's implied he helps Inferna with the dishes somehow and there is a stretchy arm on the heatmaps. His power of Elongation is also implied by his nickname, The Tall Man.
    • John Armstrong: Various characters imply that it would be really difficult to kill him normally. Inferna suggests he would be impervious to rain and other elements when he first arrives.
    • Loretta Cooke: There are many things hinting at her abilities. She appears hotter on the heat map, and she is able to cook through abnormal means (as seen from the fireless stove in the kitchen and conversation in the dining room about how she prepared the chicken).
    • Sally Meadow: There's a subtle thing at her ability during a dinner conversation where Captain Strong asks her to pass the salt and is surprised by something she does. Her testimony also reveals a lot about the limitations of her powers and implies there's some kind of movement or space transfer involved.
    • Winston Long: There's a strange back-and-forth in the conversation when he first arrives to Inferna's house where Inferna seems to say hello to herself. One can deduce that this is Winston impersonating her as a joke with his power of shapeshifting.
    • Ivy Chen: Her punny name "Polly Graf" is based on the lie detection ability she demonstrates as the investigator in the testimony levels.
    • Jenny Duncan: Her punny name "Therma Graf" is based on her body heat ability she demonstrates as the investigator in the heatmap level.
    • Owen Gutierrez: His punny name "Fono Graf" is based on the sound transcript ability he demonstrates in the conversation level.

    It turns out everyone except Inferna was in on the conspiracy!

    The main deductions to make:

    • John Armstrong was never at the party. Winston was pretending to be Armstrong anytime he appeared at the party. We never see them in the same room.
    • Anytime Winston needed to become Armstrong and vice versa, Sally would shift him outside. When fake Armstrong was going to the bathroom and Sally was getting the wine opener, they actually coordinated transporting him outside to revert to Winston. When Winston left to "take a call", he was actually transforming into Armstrong elsewhere. He reverted back to Winston during the blackout.
    • The blackout was caused by Hugh when he went to supposedly fetch Winston. You can see his arm reaching towards the stairs slightly on the heatmap before the blackout.
    • The body was transported inside the giant present, hence the poor condition of the teddy bear inside. Sally shifted the body to the right edge of the table because that was as far as she could go with her power (explained by her testimony in Testimony D). That's why the body ended up somewhere other than Armstrong's real seat.
    • The data Ivy Chen gives you is total bunk. She's in on it with the three conspirators.

    Extraction

    Extraction is really simple. The last name of the impersonator is LONG, the middle word of the blackout culprit's alias is TALL, and the person who positioned the body has first name SALLY. When read together, this makes the puzzle answer, LONG TALL SALLY.

    Author's Note

    Benji: I wasn't sure how this would play since the puzzle format was pretty experimental. I didn't have much room to build background and motivation for every character, and I was also worried that the crime would seem too contrived (which it is, to be fair). The puzzle was received very well through multiple tests, though, so I suppose people didn't really mind those things much. There wasn't a particular reason for why I decided to theme the puzzle around superheroes. The original Golden Idol game built some of its cases around figuring out the idol's powers, so I thought it would be fun to inject supernatural abilities into the mystery.

    As a little easter egg, there are actually six people in the Superhero Association photo in Inferna's kitchen. One person is obscured in the back. We had a backstory where Ivy was also part of this group and joined three of her former teammates on constructing this murder and framing. I did have concepts for what drove these characters apart years ago, but I prefer to leave it as a McGuffin of sorts.
